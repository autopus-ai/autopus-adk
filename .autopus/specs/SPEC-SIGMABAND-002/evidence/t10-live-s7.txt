Notes
-----
- The patch request ended failed:no_patch (the reply held no single diff fence; the CI evidence
  is synthetic and the fixture has no failing code), so Patch Policy item 1 refused it with no

  the requested model with model_substituted false.
- The diagnosis result reported 1 permission denial. Its tool and path were not retained,

  worktree and lists it in permission_denials.

  TestReactBandLocalPatchIT_FixtureHooksRunOutsideBand shows the git hook fixture fires outside
  band, and the claude settings use the documented settings.json, settings.local.json, and
  .mcp.json schemas.
